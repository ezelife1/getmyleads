All your questions answered ↗
What exactly am I renting?+

You rent the use of a local service website owned by Get My Leads. It is built around a particular service and area. The specific website, enquiry arrangements, monthly fee and rental terms are agreed before you start.

Is this the same as buying individual leads?+

No. Website rental gives your business use of an agreed website for a monthly fee. It is a different arrangement from paying a separate charge for each enquiry. Your proposal will explain exactly what is included.

Do I own the website or domain?+

Get My Leads retains ownership of the website and domain. You rent its use for the agreed period. Your existing business website can stay exactly where it is.

Can I see how the website is performing?+

Ask to review the available search visibility, traffic and enquiry information for the specific website. Historic results should be assessed alongside the service, area and rental cost; they do not guarantee future results.

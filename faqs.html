WEBSITE RENTAL FAQS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Understand the website rental model before deciding whether it suits your local business.

What exactly am I renting?+

You rent the use of a local service website owned by Get My Leads. It is built around a particular service and area. The specific website, enquiry arrangements, monthly fee and rental terms are agreed before you start.

Is this the same as buying individual leads?+

No. Website rental gives your business use of an agreed website for a monthly fee. It is a different arrangement from paying a separate charge for each enquiry. Your proposal will explain exactly what is included.

Do I own the website or domain?+

Get My Leads retains ownership of the website and domain. You rent its use for the agreed period. Your existing business website can stay exactly where it is.

Can I see how the website is performing?+

Ask to review the available search visibility, traffic and enquiry information for the specific website. Historic results should be assessed alongside the service, area and rental cost; they do not guarantee future results.

How much does website rental cost?+

Pricing depends on the website, service, location and rental arrangement. Tell us what you are looking for and we’ll discuss the options and monthly cost before you commit.

Will I be guaranteed a certain number of jobs?+

No. Search positions and enquiry levels can change, and winning work depends on your availability, response, quote and customer fit. We’ll explain what is known about the website rather than promise a fixed number of jobs.

Can I request an area that is not available yet?+

Yes. Send us your service and preferred area. We can check for an existing match or discuss your interest in a future website. Registering interest is not a commitment to rent.

How long is the rental agreement?+

The rental period, notice requirements and what happens when the agreement ends are set out in the proposal for that website. Review those terms before deciding whether it fits your business.

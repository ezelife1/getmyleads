THE RENTAL MODEL

What is a ranked website rental?

A ranked website rental gives a business use of a website that has been developed around a service and location, with work already put into its search visibility. The website remains owned by Get My Leads. Your business rents its use under an agreed monthly arrangement.

For example, a driveway business may be interested in a website focused on driveway enquiries in its working area. The right match depends on the services offered, the location covered and what the individual website can demonstrate.

What should you review before renting?

Look at the actual website, its relevant search visibility and any available traffic or enquiry history. A ranking for one phrase does not tell the whole story. Ask whether the searches match your services, whether the location suits you and whether you can respond to the enquiries.

We’ll talk through the available information for a specific website. There are no invented results or example sites presented as a live rental portfolio here.

What is included in the agreement?

Your proposal should identify the website and domain, the rental fee, the enquiry routing, the management included and the agreement length. It should also explain any restrictions, notice arrangements and what happens when the rental ends.

Exclusivity and service coverage are confirmed in the individual proposal. They should never be assumed from a general website description.

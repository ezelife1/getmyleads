The enquiry form

The form builds a WhatsApp message on your device using the name, business, service, area and optional details you enter. This website does not submit those form details to a separate database. WhatsApp opens so you can review the message and choose whether to send it.

When you contact Get My Leads

If you send a message or call, Get My Leads receives the information you choose to share and uses it to respond to your enquiry and discuss relevant website rental options. To ask about your information, contact Kieran on 07367 856 186.

Third-party services

WhatsApp and telephone links use services outside this website. Their own privacy practices apply when you use them. The hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and secure the site.

Cookies and analytics

This build does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or non-essential cookies. If those features are added later, this information will need to be updated.
